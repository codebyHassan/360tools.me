The History of the Classic Snake Arcade Game

First introduced as the 1976 arcade game Blockade and popularized globally on Nokia mobile devices in 1997, Snake has remained one of the most beloved casual arcade games of all time.

Top Strategies for Massive High Scores

  • Stay Near the Perimeter: Circle the edges of the board to maximize open turning space in the center.
  • Avoid Trapping Your Tail: Always plan your exit route before lunging for an apple in a narrow corridor.
  • Master the Zig-Zag Pattern: As your snake grows beyond 20 segments, snake back and forth in tight S-curves to fill the board systematically.
