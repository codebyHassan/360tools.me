Transparency & Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Tax Year 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Strict Client-Side Architecture

At 360tools.me ("we", "our", or "the Service"), we believe in privacy by design. All calculation engines (including the Section 8 Max Rent Estimator, UK Stamp Duty Calculator, 1031 Exchange Timeline Tracker, STR Cleaning Fee Splitter, and EU VAT OSS Calculator) execute entirely inside your local web browser using client-side JavaScript.

Zero Financial Data Storage

We do not transmit, collect, log, or store your input numbers, property prices, rental income, taxpayer identification, or business revenue on any server database.

2. Information We Collect

Because our tools require no user registration, email sign-up, or payment information, we collect only standard anonymous technical metadata necessary for server stability:

  • Server Logs: Standard IP addresses, browser user agent, operating system, and request timestamps to monitor infrastructure health and prevent DDoS abuse.
  • Aggregated Analytics: Anonymized page view statistics (e.g. total visits per tool) to prioritize formula updates and feature additions.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

360tools.me does not use invasive cross-site tracking cookies. We may use minimal essential cookies or browser local storage strictly to remember your preferences (such as tool toggle selections or dark mode settings). You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies at any time without degrading calculator functionality.

4. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California, you enjoy statutory data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to Know & Access: The right to know what personal data is processed. (Since we collect no personal financial data, your identity remains completely anonymous).
  • Right to Deletion: The right to request deletion of any server log entries associated with your IP.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

5. Third-Party Links & External Resources

Our calculators provide reference links to official government resources such as HUD.gov, HMRC.gov.uk, IRS.gov, and European Commission portals. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external government or third-party websites.
