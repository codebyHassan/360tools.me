Frequently Asked Questions

Why do voice options vary across different devices?

The Web Speech API accesses the natural voices installed on your operating system (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, or Google Chrome). Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome provide high-quality natural voices by default.
