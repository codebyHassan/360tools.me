Frequently Asked Questions

Why do voice options vary across different devices and browsers?

The Web Speech API accesses the natural voices installed on your operating system (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, or Google Chrome). Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome provide high-quality natural voices by default.

Can I use the downloaded audio files commercially in YouTube or Podcasts?

Yes! Because the audio is generated client-side directly on your computer or device, you own the resulting audio file for YouTube videos, podcasts, TikToks, presentations, and audiobooks.

Is my text stored or sent to any server?

No. 360tools operates with a strict 100% client-side privacy philosophy. Your text is never stored, tracked, or sent to any remote database.

Can I convert PDF documents or articles to speech?

Yes! Use our dedicated PDF to Speech Document Reader or Article to Speech Reader for instant document listening.
