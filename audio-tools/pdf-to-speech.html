Frequently Asked Questions

Are my uploaded PDF files safe and confidential?

Yes, 100%. PDF documents are rendered and processed in your local browser sandbox using client-side PDF.js. No documents are uploaded or saved to remote cloud servers, guaranteeing strict confidentiality.

Can this tool read scanned or image-only PDF documents?

The converter reads digitally encoded text layers within PDF documents. For scanned physical papers containing purely raster images, please run an OCR (Optical Character Recognition) tool first to generate a readable text layer.

Is there a page count limit for PDF reading?

No. You can open PDF files containing hundreds of pages. You can navigate through pages sequentially and synthesize audio on a per-page or multi-page basis without restrictions.
