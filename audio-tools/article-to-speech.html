Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a word count limit for long essays or book chapters?

No. Our article narrator has no word count caps, character limits, or subscription paywalls. You can paste entire 10,000-word research papers, case studies, or thesis chapters and listen to them uninterrupted.

Are my private article drafts uploaded to any external server?

Never. 360Tools uses client-side Web Speech APIs that execute locally inside your browser sandbox. Your unpublished articles, confidential proprietary briefs, and notes remain 100% private in your device RAM.

What audio formats are supported for download?

You can export the narrated article as standard universal MP3 audio, compatible with all mobile devices, podcast apps, car audio systems, and media players.
