Frequently Asked Questions

How can I use this MP3 in YouTube or TikTok videos?

Once downloaded, drag the audio file directly into your video editor (CapCut, Premiere Pro, InShot, DaVinci Resolve) as a narration or background voice track. You have 100% royalty-free commercial ownership.

What is the difference between MP3 and WAV export?

MP3 is a compressed format with small file sizes ideal for web playback, podcasts, and mobile storage. WAV is uncompressed lossless audio providing maximum acoustic fidelity for studio sound engineering and video post-production.

Does generating audio consume my internet bandwidth or mobile data?

No. Audio synthesis runs entirely inside your browser using client-side Web Audio synthesis. No data is transferred to external servers during the encoding process.
