Pro Audio Tips for High-Retention YouTube Videos

1. Optimize Words Per Minute (WPM): The standard pacing for engaging YouTube educational and entertainment commentary is between 130 and 155 words per minute. Setting speech rate to 1.05x–1.1x helps sustain audience focus without sounding rushed.

2. Use Scene Pauses Strategically: Give viewers 1.0 to 1.5 seconds of breathing room when transitioning from a dramatic hook to the introductory title card, and before presenting key statistical takeaways.

3. Layer Background Ambience: In your video editor, mix the downloaded speech track at -3 dB to -6 dB, while ducking your royalty-free background music to -18 dB to -24 dB beneath voice sections.
