About 360Tools

360Tools (360tools.me) is a collection of free online tools built to help you handle common digital tasks quickly and directly in your browser. From document handling and image compression to developer utilities and seller calculators, our goal is to keep things simple, practical, and accessible.

Our Mission

Why 360Tools exists and what drives our design

Every day, individuals and teams waste time downloading bulky software, navigating paywalls, or struggling with complicated settings just to perform basic file edits or financial calculations. We created 360Tools with a clear mission:

Accessible Directly in the Browser

Eliminate the hassle of installing standalone software for everyday tasks. Access your tools anytime on desktop, tablet, or mobile.

Simple, Intuitive Interfaces

Clean interfaces without unnecessary clutter or confusing options, allowing you to complete your task in seconds.

Focus on Privacy & Local Processing

Prioritize client-side operations so your confidential documents, scripts, and inputs remain on your device whenever technically feasible.

Speed & Lightweight Performance

Fast load times, minimal dependencies, and instant results powered by modern browser APIs and WebAssembly.

How 360Tools Works

Understanding our technical processing methods

Different tools on 360Tools use different underlying processing methods depending on their computational requirements:

Client-Side File & Media Processing

The majority of our media tools (such as PDF merging, splitting, image compression, canvas filters, and audio speech synthesis) execute directly within your browser using technologies like HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly (Wasm), Web Audio APIs, and Web Workers. In these tools, your files are processed in local device memory without being transmitted to an external server.

Local Mathematical Calculations

Calculators (including Amazon FBA, Etsy fee estimators, tax rates, and Section 8 models) run mathematical algorithms instantly in client-side JavaScript. Your numeric inputs and financial figures remain private on your device.

Server-Side Features & Third-Party Services

Certain website features—such as our editorial blog API, font delivery via Google Fonts, icon delivery via Cloudflare CDN, and standard server infrastructure logging—rely on remote network services. When interacting with any tool, we encourage users to review the individual tool's instructions and information regarding its specific workflow.

Transparent Information

We believe in honest communication. While our media and compression engines are designed for local browser execution, you should always review individual tool descriptions before handling sensitive or proprietary documents.
