How UK First-Time Buyer Stamp Duty Relief Works

Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) is a progressive tax charged on land and property transactions in England and Northern Ireland. To support first-time buyers entering the property market, HMRC applies special relief thresholds for properties priced up to £500,000.

£0

0% Tax up to £300,000

Purchases up to £300,000 incur zero Stamp Duty Land Tax for qualifying buyers.

5%

5% on Portion £300k - £500k

Pay 5% only on excess over £300k, saving up to £2,500 vs. standard home movers.

⚠️

Over £500k Cliff Edge

Frequently Asked Questions

UK Stamp Duty & Relief FAQs

How does First-Time Buyer Stamp Duty relief work in England and Northern Ireland?

First-time buyers purchasing a residential property up to £500,000 pay 0% Stamp Duty on the first £300,000, and 5% tax on the portion between £300,001 and £500,000. If the property price exceeds £500,000, all first-time buyer relief is lost completely and standard SDLT rates apply to the entire price.

What happens if a first-time buyer purchases a home for £500,001?

This is known as the 'SDLT Cliff Edge'. Exceeding the £500,000 threshold by even £1 completely revokes first-time buyer relief. The buyer must pay standard SDLT rates across all price brackets (e.g. £12,500+), whereas at £500,000 they would only pay £10,000.
