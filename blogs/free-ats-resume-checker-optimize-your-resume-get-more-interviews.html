What Is an Applicant Tracking System (ATS)?

An Applicant Tracking System is recruitment software used by employers and HR departments to manage job applications.

Recruiters may use ATS platforms to:

  • Collect and organize incoming resumes.
  • Extract candidate information and employment history.
  • Identify relevant skills and qualifications.
  • Search applications using job-related keywords.
  • Manage candidate screening and recruitment workflows.

An ATS does not necessarily reject every resume that fails to match a particular keyword. Different employers configure their recruitment systems differently, and many hiring decisions still involve human recruiters.

However, making your resume easy to read and relevant to the advertised position can improve your application quality.

How to Make Your Resume ATS-Friendly

Want to improve your chances of getting noticed by recruiters? Follow these practical resume optimization techniques.

Use Keywords from the Job Description

Every job position has different requirements.

Read the job description carefully and identify the skills, qualifications, technologies, and responsibilities mentioned by the employer.

For example, if a job requires an ASP.NET Core developer with SQL Server experience, your resume should clearly describe your relevant ASP.NET Core and SQL Server experience.

Never add skills you do not actually possess just to increase a resume score.

Choose a Simple Resume Format

Avoid unnecessary visual elements that could interfere with document parsing.

A simple structure might look like this:

  • Professional Summary
  • Technical Skills
  • Work Experience
  • Projects
  • Education
  • Certifications

Use a consistent structure throughout your document.

Write Achievement-Based Experience

Instead of writing generic responsibilities, explain what you accomplished.

Weak example:

Worked on web development projects.

Better example:

Developed ASP.NET Core Web APIs and integrated SQL Server databases to support enterprise business applications.

Where possible, include measurable results that you can genuinely support.

Use Standard Section Headings

Recruitment software may rely on familiar headings to identify information.

Prefer conventional headings such as:

  • Work Experience
  • Education
  • Technical Skills
  • Professional Summary
  • Certifications

Avoid overly creative headings that could make important information difficult to identify.

Select the Correct File Format

Follow the file-format instructions in the employer's application portal.

PDF and DOCX are common resume formats, but supported formats can vary between recruitment platforms.

Always check that your exported PDF contains selectable text rather than just a scanned image.

Common ATS Resume Mistakes to Avoid

Even experienced professionals can make resume mistakes that affect readability.

Common mistake Recommended improvement
Complex multi-column layout Use a simple layout
Missing job-related skills Add relevant, truthful keywords
Unclear job titles Use standard professional titles
Contact details inside graphics Keep details in the document body
Excessive decorative elements Choose a clean design
Inconsistent employment dates Use one date format
Generic professional summary Tailor it to the position
Spelling and grammar errors Proofread before applying

Remember that your resume must work for both recruitment software and human recruiters.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the ATS Resume Checker free?

Yes, the 360tools.me ATS Resume Checker is available as a free online tool. Visit the tool page to start analyzing your resume.

What is a good ATS resume score?

There is no universal ATS score accepted by every employer. Different recruitment systems use different configurations, criteria, and screening workflows. Treat an online score as a guide for improving your resume rather than an official hiring result.

Can an ATS read PDF resumes?

Many recruitment platforms support PDF resumes, although the exact supported formats depend on the employer's software and configuration. Always follow the application instructions.

How can I improve my ATS resume score?

Start by matching your resume to the job description, using relevant skills and keywords naturally, improving document readability, and organizing your experience under standard headings.

Are two-column resumes ATS-friendly?

Some systems can process certain multi-column layouts, but complex columns, tables, and text boxes may cause parsing problems. A simple single-column format is generally a safer choice.

Should I use the same resume for every job?

It is better to maintain a master resume and create tailored versions for relevant job applications. Highlight the experience and skills most closely related to each position.

Does a high ATS score guarantee an interview?

No. A resume score cannot guarantee an interview, job offer, or successful ATS screening. Employers also consider experience, qualifications, competition, and their individual recruitment criteria.

Is an ATS-friendly resume also suitable for human recruiters?

Yes. A well-structured resume with clear headings, readable formatting, relevant experience, and concise achievements is useful for both automated document processing and human review.
