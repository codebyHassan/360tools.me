Printify vs Printful vs Gelato: 2026 Profit Margin Breakdown

Detailed pricing analysis, production routing, international shipping speeds, and break-even subscription points.

Printify Advantage

Printify operates a distributed network of independent print providers (Monster Digital, SwiftPOD, Dimona Tee). This creates aggressive price competition, making standard apparel blanks up to $2.00 cheaper per unit.

Printful Quality

Printful owns its fulfillment facilities directly in the US and Europe. While blank base prices are slightly higher, they offer consistent color profiles, custom inside neck labels, and seamless branding packs.

Gelato Global Speed

Gelato routes orders to over 100 localized production hubs across 32 countries. For posters, acrylics, and calendars, local production slashes cross-border customs delays and international shipping fees.
