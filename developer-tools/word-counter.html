Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is calculating reading time and speaking time important?

Reading time gives readers a psychological roadmap before engaging with an article (e.g., "5 min read"), significantly reducing page bounce rates. Speaking time helps podcasters, public speakers, and video creators time their scripts precisely.

What is an optimal keyword density for SEO copywriting?

Modern search engines recommend a keyword density of 1% to 2.5% for your primary search query. Anything above 3% to 4% risks triggering Google's keyword stuffing spam penalty.
