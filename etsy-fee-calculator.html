How to Beat the Offsite Ads Trap

If your shop does less than $10,000 in trailing 12-month revenue, you can opt out of Offsite Ads in your Shop Manager settings. If you remain enrolled, a 15% surcharge is taken on the full order value (including shipping). Ensure your gross profit margin is at least 45% to prevent ad-attributed orders from triggering a loss.

Free Shipping vs. Calculated Shipping

Etsy charges the 6.5% transaction fee on both the item price and the shipping charged to the buyer. Offering "Free Shipping Guarantee" for orders over $35 boosts search rank, but you must bake average packaging & courier expenses into your item retail base.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the $0.20 listing fee get charged?

The listing fee is charged when you publish a new product, when a product sells and auto-renews (if quantity > 1), or when a 4-month listing expires and is renewed.

How does Etsy calculate transaction fees on multiple quantities?

If a customer buys 3 units of a $20 item ($60 total) + $5 shipping, Etsy charges 6.5% on the entire $65 ($4.23 transaction fee) plus a $0.20 auto-renewal listing fee for the 2 extra units sold.

How does the 2.5% currency conversion fee apply?

If your payment bank account is in Canadian Dollars (CAD) or Euros (EUR), but you list your items in US Dollars (USD), Etsy takes a 2.5% currency conversion fee on the gross sales balance before depositing to your bank. Always list in your shop's primary bank currency to save this 2.5%.
