Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why should I not divide my salary by 2,080 hours (40 hrs/wk * 52 wks)?

As a full-time employee, your employer covers payroll taxes, healthcare, paid time off, and equipment. Freelancers spend 35% to 50% of their working hours on unpaid admin, marketing, and client pitches. Dividing by 2,080 will severely underprice your services.
