1

Pass-Through vs. Profit

Frequently Asked Questions

STR Cleaning & Payout FAQs

Should property management commission be calculated on the cleaning fee?

Industry best practice dictates that property management commission should be calculated strictly on the Nightly Accommodation Rate, because the cleaning fee is a 100% pass-through expense paid to cleaning vendors.

Does Airbnb charge their 3% host platform fee on the cleaning fee?

Yes. Airbnb deducts the 3% standard host service fee from the entire booking total, including both the nightly rate and the cleaning fee.
