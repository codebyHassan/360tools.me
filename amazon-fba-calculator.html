The 0.75-Inch Thickness Cliff

If a product's thickness exceeds 0.75 inches, even by 0.05 inches, it immediately jumps from Small Standard to Large Standard, triggering a steep jump in fulfillment base fees. Vacuum sealing and custom cardboard sleeves can keep your product in the lowest fee bracket.

Why Divisor 139 Matters

Commercial carriers (FedEx/UPS retail) frequently use a 166 divisor, but Amazon enforces the aggressive 139 divisor for US fulfillment. A lightweight pillow or bulky jacket measuring 18" × 14" × 6" yields a dimensional weight of $(18 \times 14 \times 6) / 139 = 10.88\text{ lbs}$, even if it only weighs 1 lb.
