Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting 360Tools and our utility suite.

Can I request a specific online tool?

Yes! We actively build new tools based on community suggestions. Use the form above, select "Request a New Tool", and specify the formulas or workflows you need.

Is there any paid tier or subscription on 360Tools?

No. All 30+ tools on 360tools.me are 100% free with zero paywalls, zero watermarks, and no mandatory registration.

How do I report an incorrect calculation or bug?

Select "Report a Bug", describe which tool you were using, your input values, and your expected result. Our engineering team will review the algorithm promptly.

How are my files and calculations protected?

Every tool on 360tools.me executes locally in your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio, and WebAssembly. Nothing is ever uploaded to our servers.
