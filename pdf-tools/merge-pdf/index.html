Why Merge PDF Files in Your Web Browser?

Traditional online PDF mergers require you to upload private documents to remote cloud servers, wait for server-side processing queues, and download the output over your internet connection. This creates privacy risks for confidential bank records, real estate contracts, and medical forms. 360Tools changes this paradigm by executing the entire PDF concatenation process directly inside your device's memory using modern HTML5 and JavaScript libraries.
