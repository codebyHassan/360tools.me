360Tools uses WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript to merge your documents locally in your web browser. Your confidential contracts, invoices, and personal files are never uploaded to our servers or stored in the cloud.

Why Merge PDF Files in Your Web Browser?

Traditional online PDF mergers require you to upload private documents to remote cloud servers, wait for server-side processing queues, and download the output over your internet connection. This creates privacy risks for confidential bank records, real estate contracts, and medical forms. 360Tools changes this paradigm by executing the entire PDF concatenation process directly inside your device's memory using modern HTML5 and JavaScript libraries.

Key Advantages of 360Tools Merger

  • Zero Data Uploads: Files never leave your local machine.
  • Drag & Drop Ordering: Reorder files easily before merging.
  • Unlimited Usage: No daily limits or page count limits.
  • Cross-Device: Works smoothly on desktop, iPhone, iPad, and Android.

Supported Files & Limitations

  • Standard PDF 1.3 to PDF 2.0 documents.
  • Preserves bookmarks, vector fonts, and high-res photos.
  • Encrypted/password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before merging.
