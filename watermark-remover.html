Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to select a watermark for clean removal?

For rectangular logos, stamps, or date-time overlays, use the Box Selection Tool and drag the handles so the box just barely encloses the watermark. For irregular shapes or handwriting, switch to the Brush Eraser and paint directly over the pixels you wish to erase.

Which inpainting method should I choose?

Use Content-Aware Texture Inpaint for photos with detailed background textures (sky, grass, fabric). For solid or smooth gradient backgrounds, choose Border Gradient Diffusion. For TV stamps or subtitles, use Delogo Smooth Blur.

Are output downloads labeled with 360tools.me branding?

Yes! All processed images and videos are cleanly exported with filenames starting with 360tools.me_watermark_removed_... with no watermarks added by our tool.
