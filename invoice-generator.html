Frequently Asked Questions — Free Invoice Generator

Everything you need to know about creating, saving, and downloading PDF invoices for free.

Is my client & financial data private?

Yes, 100%. All form entries, total calculations, line items, and uploaded company logos execute purely inside your web browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored on any external server.

How do I export my invoice to PDF?

Simply click the "Print / Save PDF" button at the top (or press Ctrl+P). In your browser's print options menu, set the printer destination to "Save as PDF".

Can I change currencies and add tax?

Yes! Use the settings panel on the left to pick your preferred currency ($, €, £, ₨, ₹, etc.), set custom tax percentages, apply discounts, or include shipping fees.

Will my uploaded logo print cleanly?

Yes. Uploaded logos are converted into Base64 format locally in your browser memory. This guarantees that your logo renders crisp and clear during PDF export without broken image URLs.
