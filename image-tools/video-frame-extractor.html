Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract frames from 4K (2160p) and 60FPS videos?

Yes! Since the processing is powered by your browser's native hardware decoder and canvas engine, 4K, 1080p, and high-framerate 60FPS videos can be extracted at full original fidelity.

What is the difference between PNG and JPEG extraction?

PNG produces 100% lossless images without compression artifacts, ideal for graphic design, thumbnail creation, and visual analysis. JPEG produces much smaller file sizes with adjustable quality (e.g. 90%), perfect when extracting hundreds of frames.

Can I capture a single frame snapshot at a specific second?

Yes. Use the video player controls or the -1 / +1 frame step buttons to find your exact desired moment, then click the "Snapshot Frame" button to capture and add it to your gallery instantly.

Is there any limit on video duration or file size?

There are no artificial server limits or paywalls. The only limit is your device's available RAM memory. If you are working with very long videos (over 30 minutes), we recommend using the Time Interval mode or Trim Range feature.
