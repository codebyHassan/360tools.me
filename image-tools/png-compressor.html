Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Standard PNG and WebP Alpha mode?

Standard PNG mode performs lossless palette optimization while outputting a traditional .png file. WebP Alpha mode converts your graphic into Google's modern WebP format, preserving identical transparency at significantly smaller byte sizes for faster web delivery.

Will transparent PNG compression create fuzzy borders around text?

No. Our quantizer preserves anti-aliased edge pixels, guaranteeing crisp text and sharp icon outlines against dark and light website backgrounds alike.
