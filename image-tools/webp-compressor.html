Frequently Asked Questions

Why is WebP better than JPEG and PNG for web development?

WebP combines the photographic richness of JPEG with the alpha transparency of PNG at 25%–35% lower file sizes. Google Search actively rewards sites utilizing WebP with higher PageSpeed performance scores.

What browser compatibility does WebP enjoy today?

WebP is universally supported across 97%+ of global web browsers, including Google Chrome, Apple Safari (iOS & macOS), Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Opera.
