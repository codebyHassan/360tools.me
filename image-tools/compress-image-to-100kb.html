Frequently Asked Questions

Why do government application portals enforce a 100KB image limit?

Public service portals process millions of applicants simultaneously. Capping images at 100KB minimizes server storage overhead, prevents database bottlenecks, and accelerates page load times for low-bandwidth mobile connections.

Will my image become pixelated or blurry after compressing to 100KB?

No. Standard passport and headshot dimensions (such as 600x600 pixels or 2x2 inches) compress exceptionally well into 60KB–95KB without visible artifacts or loss of print resolution.

What if my portal requires a 200KB or 500KB file instead?

You can instantly switch to our dedicated 200KB Compressor or 500KB Compressor for higher resolution requirements.
